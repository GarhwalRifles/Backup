Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the ehydromet Backup Tool ("the App") handles information. The App is a private, personal project and is not offered as a service to the public.

1. Who We Are

The App is developed and used by a single individual for personal, non-commercial purposes. There are no third-party users.

2. What Data the App Accesses

The App uses Google OAuth 2.0 with the scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive or https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file in order to:

  • Create new backup files in the developer's own Google Drive account.
  • List files it has previously created (to manage old backups).

The App does not read, modify, delete, or transmit any other files or data stored in the user's Google account.

3. How Data Is Stored

Backup files are stored only in the developer's private Google Drive folder. OAuth credentials (client ID, client secret, and refresh token) are stored locally on the developer's own server and are never shared.

4. Data Sharing

No data collected or accessed by this App is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party. The App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. Data Retention

Backup files remain in Google Drive until the developer manually deletes them. No copy of any user data is retained outside of Google Drive.

6. Security

All communication with Google APIs is performed over HTTPS. Refresh tokens are stored with restricted file permissions on the developer's server.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes.
